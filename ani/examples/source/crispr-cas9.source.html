Molecular Biology & Structural Sourcing

  • Crystal Structure Basis: Modeled from Streptococcus pyogenes Cas9 in complex with single-guide RNA and target DNA (PDB accession 4UN3, 2.5 Å resolution, Nishimasu et al., Cell 2014; and 5F9R, Jiang et al., Science 2016).
  • Allosteric Conformational Mechanism: Real-time single-molecule FRET and cryo-EM trajectories demonstrate that complete 20-nt R-loop propagation acts as an allosteric switch, driving an ~180° rotation (~30 Å relocation) of the HNH catalytic domain from an inactive conformational checkpoint onto the target strand scissile phosphate (Sternberg et al., Nature 2014; Dagdas et al., Science 2017).
  • Seed Mismatch & Cleavage Gating: Mutations in the PAM-proximal seed region (positions 1–8) stall R-loop propagation, preventing REC3 lobe distortion and trapping the HNH domain in an inactive state, terminating off-target DNA cleavage (Szczelkun et al., PNAS 2014).
  • Catalytic Cleavage Coordinates: Dual-nicking mechanism: RuvC active site (Asp10, Glu762, His983, Asp986) cleaves the non-target strand at -3 relative to PAM; HNH active site (Asp839, His840, Asn863) cleaves the target strand at -3 relative to PAM, producing a blunt double-strand break (DSB) (Jinek et al., Science 2012).
