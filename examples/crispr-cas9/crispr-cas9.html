Sources and limits

  • Crystal structures: Streptococcus pyogenes Cas9–sgRNA–PAM DNA complex (PDB 4UN3, 2.59 Å, Anders et al., Nature 2014) and Cas9 R-loop complex primed for cleavage (PDB 5F9R, Jiang et al., Science 2016).
  • Dual-strand cleavage: HNH (Asp839, His840, Asn863) cleaves the target strand and RuvC (Asp10, Glu762, His983, Asp986) cleaves the non-target strand, each 3 nt upstream of the 5'-NGG-3' PAM, generating a predominantly blunt double-strand break (Jinek et al., Science 2012, doi:10.1126/science.1225829).
  • HNH conformational checkpoint: Complete 20-nt R-loop propagation drives an allosteric transition moving HNH (~180° rotation, ~30 Å displacement) from an inactive checkpoint into the catalytic conformation (Sternberg et al., Nature 2015, PMID 26524520; Dagdas et al., Science Advances 2017, doi:10.1126/sciadv.aao0027).
  • Seed-mismatch stalling: PAM-proximal seed mismatches (positions 1–8) stall directional R-loop zippering and trap HNH in the inactive checkpoint, preventing cleavage (Szczelkun et al., PNAS 2014).
  • What is simplified (illustrative): Schematic cross-section; domain outlines, the 9 s cycle duration, and the exact stall at 6/20 bp in the mismatch state are teaching simplifications rather than measured kinetics or atomic coordinates.
  • Educational use only: Explains molecular mechanism only. Not clinical guidance, gene-editing protocol, or therapeutic advice.
