Model. BendLab (MIT licence), github.com/Yash-Tripath1/Bendlab, sim.py: τ·dr/dt = k·sin(φ − θ) − r, r clipped to ±max rate, dθ/dt = r, forward Euler with a one-minute step, θ and r starting at zero. In the code r is in degrees per hour, so k is also in degrees per hour as written. BendLab describes the form as related to the Cholodny–Went hypothesis and the Blaauw sine law. The model is phenomenological: it has no auxin state and does not simulate hormone transport.

Data BendLab fits. Stem-angle trajectories from S13 Data of Multiple light signaling pathways control solar tracking in sunflowers, PLOS Biology (2023), doi 10.1371/journal.pbio.3002344, as reported in the BendLab README. This figure does not display those data.

Fitted presets. The three “fit” buttons load parameter values supplied by BendLab’s author for seedlings from the published data (Brooks et al. 2023, Fig 1C, as reported by the author): a typical plant (k = 18.5 °/h, τ = 3.63 h, max rate = 20.7 °/h, φ₀ = 49.1°); a plant whose fitted cap is not reached (k = 22.9 °/h, τ = 4.17 h, max rate = 6.2 °/h, φ₀ = 31.7°); and plant ZT3-8, where the cap binds (k = 40.8 °/h, τ = 4.37 h, max rate = 7.4 °/h, φ₀ = 45.3°). Every plant runs from rest (θ = 0, r = 0) in static light for twelve hours, the protocol of the original fits. Under it, the second plant peaks at 5.9 °/h, under its 6.2 °/h cap, and ZT3-8 would peak near 12 °/h without a cap, so its 7.4 °/h cap bites. The author reports (personal communication, 2026-10-16) that across all 48 fitted seedlings the fitted cap binds for exactly one plant. The figure runs the model with these values; it does not plot the measured trajectories.

What the rate-limit note shows. BendLab reports that max rate cannot be identified from a trajectory that never reaches the limit (its README gives an example fit with identical error with and without the limit). The blue curve and the status line reproduce that idea for the sliders you choose; they are not BendLab's fitted results. BendLab's results are the author's own and not peer reviewed.
