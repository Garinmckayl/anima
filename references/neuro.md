# Neurobiology figures

Show the correct direction of information flow and distinguish anatomical compartments: dendrites, soma, axon hillock/initial segment, axon, myelin internodes, nodes of Ranvier and synaptic terminal where relevant. Do not imply that every neuron has the same morphology or myelination.

For action-potential figures, show propagation along the membrane and distinguish the local depolarization from saltatory conduction in a myelinated axon. Label the membrane potential convention and cite resting, threshold, peak and timing values if shown. Values vary with neuron type, species, temperature and recording conditions; avoid treating a single trace as universal.

For synapses, distinguish presynaptic vesicle release, transmitter diffusion across the cleft, postsynaptic receptors and signal termination. Do not depict vesicle release as direct electrical continuity between cells.

Use `references/bio.md` for sourcing and scale. A mechanism can be simplified, but make omissions explicit and keep the direction and sequence biologically faithful.
